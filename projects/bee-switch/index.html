Bee Switch 🐝

A daily word puzzle built from the New York Times Spelling Bee, and a sister to Bee Definition. You are given a stack of words from one day's Spelling Bee, one for every letter of that day's pangram — the word that uses all seven letters.

Slide the words left and right, and shuffle them up and down, until the shaded middle column spells a pangram reading top to bottom.

Click any letter to pull it into the middle column. Use ▲ and ▼ to move a word up or down the stack.

Every puzzle has a name, shown in the heading below: today's is the date, and a random puzzle gets a number. Type a name in to play that exact puzzle, which is also how you set a friend the same one.
