Tetronimo Place 🧱

A Tetris variant. Fill the board using only the two tetronimo shapes provided.

Click/tap on a cell border to place a line there, click/tap on a line to remove it.

Once lines are placed to divide the board into one of provided tetronimo shapes, the cells within that shape will be colored in.

Additionally, the board has × and = marks, and hatched cells.

  • An × indicates this is a border between two different tetronimo pieces, and the two pieces are different shapes.
  • An = indicates this is a border between two different tetronimo pieces, and the two pieces are the same shape.
  • No single tetronimo piece may cover more than one hatched cell.

Board

The board comes in three sizes: small (6x6), medium (8x8), and large (10x10).
