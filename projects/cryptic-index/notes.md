- **Container indicators:**
    - !r Put one set of letters inside another! Like positions words expect they say to put collecton of letters inside or around other letters. 
    - Within (insert one set of letters into another): in, into, within, packed with, is in, hides, holds, contains, including
    - Around (wrap one word around another): about, around, outside, bracing
    - Active containment: Some words can "eat" fodder to put the fodder inside themselves. Or they can "wear" fodder to put fodder around them, eat, consume, grab, absorb, embrace, wear, put on, enter, pierce, interrupt, capture, devours
    - X takes Y means put Y inside X?
    - Guzzling
    - Renovated with
    - Outside
    - X about Y means put Y in X
    - Caught in
    - X to grab Y (Y goes inside X)

- Deletion Indicator:
    - !r Can look like anagram indicators. Like selectors, but just what you don't select.
    - Delete from positions:
        - Curtail (ends), faceless (starts), heatless (middle), gutted (outside), principals quit (firsts)
    - Delete by name: X out of Y (remove X or synonym of, out of Y), aimless, powerless
    - X away from (remove X)
    - Without any directions (remove N/E/S/W)
    - X left Y (remove X from Y)
    - offended (remove "end", last)
    - X removed from Y (literally)
    - trailer released (remove last letter)
    - Hopin' (implies you will take some word and remove the last letter/g)
    - X is banned (remove X)
    - Breaking (remove first)
    - X Dispensed by Y (remove Y from X)
    - Calorie-free (remove "cal")
    - Not initially
    - Topless
    - Incomplete (remove last letter)
    - Avoid Northern (remove N)
    - No time (remove T)
    - Defying the odds (remove the odd letters)
