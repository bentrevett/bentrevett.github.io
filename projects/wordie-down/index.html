Wordie Down ⬇️

A daily word puzzle, a downward variant of Wordie Grid. You are given five words, one per row, and one shaded square in each row.

Read the shaded squares from top to bottom and they spell a word running downwards. Reorder the five words so that they do. The shaded squares never move — they belong to the rows, not to the words — so a word gives up a different letter depending on which row it lands in.

There is more than one word hiding in there, and you are after all of them. Every word, across and down, comes from the same list of common five letter words, and no down word is ever one of the five words across.

Click a word to pick it up, then click another to swap the two. Click it again to put it down. The shaded squares turn green when they spell one of the words you are looking for.
