Wordie Place 🧩

A word puzzle. Eight five letter words are randomly chosen, we give you their middle letters only. The remaining bigrams (first two and last two letters) become tiles. Place each tile into the table to build eight valid five letter words.

Click/tap a tile to select it, then click/tap on a space before/after a middle letter to place it. Click/tap on a placed tile to reset it.

A red cross indicates the word is invalid. A green tick indicates the word is valid.

You do not have to find the eight words used to construct the puzzle, any valid set of eight words will be a valid solve, hence there are potentially more than one solution to each puzzle.
