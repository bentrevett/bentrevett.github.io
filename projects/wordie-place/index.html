Wordie Place 🧩

A word puzzle. Four, six, or eight (depending on the size of the board) five letter words are randomly chosen, we give you their middle letters only. The remaining bigrams (first two and last two letters) of those words become tiles. Place each tile into the board to build a valid five letter word on every line.

Click/tap a tile to select it, then click/tap on a space before/after a middle letter to place it. Click/tap on a placed tile to reset it.

A red cross indicates the word is invalid. A green tick indicates the word is valid.

You do not have to find the words used to construct the puzzle, any valid set of words will be a valid solve, hence there are potentially more than one solution to each puzzle.

Board

The board comes in three sizes: small (four words), medium (six words), and large (eight words).
