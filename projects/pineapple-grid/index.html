Pineapple Grid 🍍

A daily solo puzzle, a grid variant of Pineapple Lines.

You have a five by five grid, with the four corners dealt face up to start. Over seven turns you are dealt five cards, place three of them into any empty cells and discard the other two. After seven turns all twenty five cells are full.

Every row and every column is scored as a five card poker hand — ten hands in all, with no diagonals and no multipliers. Each card therefore counts twice, once in its row and once in its column. Everybody gets the same cards in the same order each day.

Select a card, then click an empty cell to place it.

Every puzzle has a name, shown in the heading below: today's is the date, and a random puzzle gets a number. Type a name in to play that exact puzzle, which is also how you set a friend the same one.
